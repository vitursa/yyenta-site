Yahrzeit Yenta

You'll never hear the end of it.

Enter your yahrzeits once. The yenta remembers the rest.

  • The right Hebrew date, every year.Enter the Hebrew date, or just the English one and whether it was after sunset. She works out the Hebrew date for you, even the tricky leap-year ones.
  • As many reminders as you want.A week before, erev Shabbos, the evening it begins, or any day you pick. Set one or set five, for each yahrzeit.
  • Kaddish, covered.Mark the yahrzeits you say kaddish for, and every reminder for them says so.
  • Bring your list.Paste it, open a spreadsheet, or let her find the yahrzeits already in your calendar.
  • Share with the family.Pick the yahrzeits, pick who gets them. They add them with one tap. ...Okay, maybe it's two taps. Is one more tap going to kill you?
